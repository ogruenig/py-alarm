Files to Upload to ESP32:
==========================
Upload all files from src/ and optionally tests/ to your ESP32.

Minimum required for operation:
- src/config.py
- src/tm1637.py
- src/ds3231.py
- src/dfplayer.py
- src/rotary_encoder.py
- src/main.py

Total Size: ~28 KB (fits easily on ESP32)
